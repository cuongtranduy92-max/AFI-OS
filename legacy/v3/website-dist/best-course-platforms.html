The tools in detail

Skool

Community & courses$99 / month

Best for: Running a paid community and a course in one place, without stitching tools together.

What works

  • Community, courses and payments in a single product
  • Flat pricing — no per-member tier jumps
  • Very small learning curve for members

What does not

  • Limited design control over the member area
  • No advanced course features such as SCORM or quizzes at depth
  • Single flat price is expensive for a very small community

Verdict: Strong when the community is the product. Weak if you mainly need a course platform with detailed learning features.

Thinkific

Course platformFree – $199 / month

Best for: Publishing structured online courses with quizzes, drip content and certificates.

What works

  • Genuinely usable free plan for a first course
  • Quizzes, assignments and completion certificates included
  • Own domain and full page customisation

What does not

  • Community features are thin compared with dedicated tools
  • Transaction fee on the free plan
  • Email marketing needs a separate tool

Verdict: The safer choice when the course itself is the product and you need learning features rather than a social feed.

How I tested

Each tool was given the same source material and the same amount of time. I paid for the plans myself where a trial was not enough to reach a real result. Nothing here is based on a vendor demo.

Where should I host and sell my course?

There is no single answer that fits everyone, and any page telling you otherwise is selling something. Match the tool to what you already have: the shape of your source material, how often you publish, and how much control you actually need. The comparison above is written so you can rule tools out, which is usually faster than trying to rule one in.
